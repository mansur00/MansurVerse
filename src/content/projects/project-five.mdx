## Overview

A collaborative task management workspace built for distributed engineering and design teams who prioritize velocity and focus.

## Problem

Context switching between heavy modal dialogs and nested navigation trees caused cognitive friction during daily planning sessions.

## Solution

Designed command-palette shortcuts, collapsible split panels, and flat-fill rows with clear status pills and accessible focus rings.

## Results

Teams completed sprint planning tasks 80% faster, reporting significantly reduced visual distraction and full keyboard accessibility.
