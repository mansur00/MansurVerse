## Overview

Generic overview describing Project Six, an experimental microsite celebrating digital craft and generative visual motion.

## Problem

High visual fidelity often degraded frame rates on mobile hardware and excluded users with reduced motion preferences.

## Solution

Engineered hardware-accelerated shaders with low draw-call overhead, pairing them with an immediate zero-motion fallback.

## Results

Achieved locked 60fps across low-power phones while maintaining full accessibility and keyboard traversability.
