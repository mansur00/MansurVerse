## Overview

Generic overview describing Project Two, a modern analytics dashboard tailored for mobile and tablet touch interactions.

## Problem

Complex data tables and chart filters overwhelmed small-screen users, leading to high drop-off during onboarding and task completion.

## Solution

Streamlined mobile flows into progressive disclosure rows, introduced clear tactile gestures, and established high-contrast token pairings for dark and light environments.

## Results

User engagement grew by 65% in the first quarter post-launch, while negative user reviews related to navigation were eliminated.
